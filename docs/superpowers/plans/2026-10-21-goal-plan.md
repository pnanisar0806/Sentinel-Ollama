# Goal tracker and goal plan Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:subagent-driven-development. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax. Every subagent runs on Opus (owner rule).

**Goal:** The owner can see, every month, whether the house, FI, emergency and education goals are on track. The owner also gets a goal plan beside the unchanged normal plan, proposing more money or more equity on new money, with a risk disclosure. The monthly advisor waits for the owner's budget update, up to the 7th.

**Architecture:**
- Pure projection math lives in `src/domain/goal-math.ts`, which imports no goal status.
- `src/domain/goal-plan.ts` is the only new reader of goal status. It loads the tracker and builds the goal plan, and only web code calls it.
- The plan acts only by proposing existing 48-hour rail changes: `monthly_budget_paise`, `tactical_monthly_paise`, and a new `goal_equity_ceiling_pct` rail. The rebalance engine reads that rail and never goal status.
- The advisor hold extends `monthlyAdvice` in `src/jobs/report.ts`.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, vitest + PGlite, postgres-js/Supabase, Next.js `web/`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-21-goal-plan-design.md`, owner-approved 2026-10-21. **§11 and §12 override earlier sections.** Specifically:
- the §2 2026-10-21 row that counted projected vests is superseded;
- the §3 phrases "shortfall after expected vests" and "no vest data" are superseded: there are no projected vests;
- the §3 non-goal "no change to how the normal plan … advises" is superseded by Q4 (timing only) and by §11.2 (the signed ceiling rail).

## Global Constraints

- Money is never a float: branded `bigint` paise only. Growth rates are integer basis points; per-month factors are integer micros. Round shortfalls **up** to the next rupee and projected balances **down**.
- An unknown is `null`, never `0n`, and renders as `unknown` or `—`, never `₹0`.
- Absent `bucket_flows` for a goal means ₹0 moved, which is a known zero (spec §2, emergency fund row). This is different from an unknown price.
- All projections are in real terms: real rate = nominal − 6% (simple subtraction, as the spec states). The rates are:

  | class | real rate | sensitivity |
  |---|---|---|
  | equity | 6% | ±3 |
  | debt | 1% | ±2 |
  | gold | 2% | ±2 |
  | cash | −3.5% (savings accounts pay 2.5% nominal: SBI and HDFC, both flat since June 2025; owner, 2026-10-21) | ±0 |

  Contribution growth is 10% nominal = 4% real a year.
- "This month" means the IST calendar month (`istMonth` and `istDate` from `src/domain/sips.ts`).
- `goal-plan.ts` may import `funded-status.ts` and `buckets.ts`. No normal-plan entry point may reach `goal-plan.ts` (Task 2).
- No goal-plan code path creates an order, sells, or writes anything except through `proposeRailChange`.
- Never widen a test band or edit an expected constant to turn a test green. Derive expected values from fixture rows.
- The `Db` interface is `{ query<T>(sql, params?): Promise<T[]>; exec(sql); withTransaction<T>(fn); close() }`. PGlite returns `date` and `timestamptz` as `Date`; postgres-js returns strings. Select `::text` where exactness matters.
- Commit locally only. Never push. Messages end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 (1M context) <noreply@anthropic.com>`.

## Review Focus

1. **Stale RSU price.** If no `prices_eod` row for the RSU instrument is within 7 days of now, or the RSU quantity is unknown, the house balance and verdict are `null` ("unknown"). They are never B2 alone and never "on track" (Task 2).
2. **Double counting.** FI corpus excludes RSU-kind positions and the B2, B3 and B4 balances. The sum of the four goal balances must never exceed total assets plus the fresh RSU value minus the stale RSU position (Task 2).
3. **A goal already met:** the verdict is "on track", the shortfall is 0, and no "raise budget" button appears for that goal (Tasks 3 and 7).
4. **Budget hold across the 7th.**
   - On the 8th IST the advisor runs with no confirmation.
   - A budget change proposed on the 6th that is still in its 48 hours holds the advisor even on the 8th, until it activates (*my call*, so advice is never sized on a budget about to change).
   - A confirmation recorded last month does not count this month (Task 5).
5. **Revoking the ceiling** while equity sits between 60% and the signed ceiling. The engine then sees equity over 60%, and its existing dilute-first rule applies: no sale while new money can close the gap. A test pins that the revocation alone produces no TRIM when the excess is under `SELL_TOLERANCE` (Task 4).

**Interfaces (Produces):**
- `type Mix = { equityBps: number; debtBps: number; goldBps: number }`. The three parts sum to 10 000.
- `type Rates = { equityBps: number; debtBps: number; goldBps: number; cashBps: number }`. These are annual real rates; `cashBps` is negative.
- `realRates(scenario: 'central' | 'pessimistic' | 'optimistic'): Rates`, derived from ASSUMPTIONS. For central: `{600, 100, 200, -350}`. Cash has no sensitivity band.
- `newMoneyMix(equityCeilingPct: number): Mix`:
  - equity = the ceiling;
  - gold = `goalGoldMixPct`;
  - debt = the rest.
  - It throws if the ceiling is above 92.5 or below 0.
- `interface Pots { equity: bigint; debt: bigint; gold: bigint; cash: bigint }`, in paise. New money never goes to cash.
- `project(start: Pots, opts: { months: number; monthlyPaise: bigint; mix: Mix; growthBps: number; rates: Rates; shockAtMonth?: number }): Pots`:
  - Monthly steps. Each pot grows by `annualBps / 12` applied monthly in integer micros, floored.
  - Then the month's contribution is split by `mix`.
  - The contribution steps up by `growthBps` every 12 months from month 0.
  - When `shockAtMonth` is set, the equity pot drops by `crashEquityDrop` at the end of that month.
- `potsTotal(p: Pots): bigint`.
- `levelMonthlyToClose(gapPaise: bigint, months: number, annualBps: number): bigint`: the flat monthly amount, with no step-up, whose future value at that rate reaches `gapPaise`. Round up to the rupee. It returns 0n when the gap ≤ 0, and the whole gap when months ≤ 0.
- `firstMonthReaching(start: Pots, target: bigint, opts: Omit<ProjectOpts,'months'>, maxMonths = 1200): number | null`: the month index at which the total reaches the target, or null.

- [ ] **Tests, red first:**
  - `project` with zero rates and no growth equals start + months × contribution, exactly.
  - One year of a ₹1,00,000 equity pot at central rates with no contribution is ≥ ₹1,06,000 and < ₹1,06,200 (12 monthly steps of 0.5%), and floors.
  - The contribution step-up applies at month 12, not month 11: a fixture with growth 1 000 bps (10%) shows the 13th contribution 10% larger.
  - `shockAtMonth: 0` halves the equity pot and leaves debt and gold untouched.
  - A cash pot shrinks in real terms: ₹1,00,000 for 12 months at central rates is < ₹1,00,000 and > ₹96,000.
  - `newMoneyMix(60)` gives `{6000, 3250, 750}`. `newMoneyMix(93)` throws.
  - `levelMonthlyToClose`:
    - the result, fed back through `project` over the same months and rate, reaches the gap;
    - the result minus ₹1 does not reach it;
    - the edge cases are 0 gap and 0 months.
  - `firstMonthReaching` returns null when the target is unreachable within `maxMonths`.
  - **Mutation check:** making `levelMonthlyToClose` round down must turn the "reaches the gap" test red.

**Interfaces (Produces):**
```ts
type GoalId = 'FI' | 'HOUSE' | 'EMERGENCY' | 'EDUCATION';
interface GoalCard {
  id: GoalId; name: string; targetPaise: bigint; targetLabel: string;   // e.g. "₹65L real by 2033"
  balancePaise: bigint | null;          // null = unknown (Review Focus 1)
  balanceParts: { label: string; paise: bigint | null }[]; // e.g. B2 flows, RSU 78 sh @ ₹x (2026-10-20)
  projectedPaise: bigint | null;        // central, at the target date
  verdict: 'ON_TRACK' | 'SHORT' | 'NOT_STARTED' | 'UNKNOWN';
  shortMonthlyPaise: bigint | null;     // 0n when on track
  finishYear: { central: number | null; pessimistic: number | null; optimistic: number | null };
  assumptions: string[];                // rendered verbatim on the card
  reason: string | null;                // why unknown
}
interface GoalTracker { asOf: string; monthlyBudgetPaise: bigint; cards: GoalCard[]; fiPots: Pots | null }
loadGoalTracker(db: Db, nowIso: string): Promise<GoalTracker>
```

**Rules:**

- **FI.**
  - `fiPots` sums the `loadPositions` values by asset class. It excludes `kind === 'RSU'` positions. CASH is its own pot.
  - The B2 + B3 + B4 flow balances are then subtracted from cash first (bucket money sits in bank accounts until invested), then debt, then equity, never below zero.
  - Contributions: `monthly_budget_paise`, split by `newMoneyMix(60)`, plus `epfMonthlyContributionInr` into debt. Both grow 4% real a year.
    - The EPF line is a second `project` contribution stream. Implement it by projecting twice and summing, or by extending `project` with an extra stream; either is fine.
  - Target: `computeFICorpusBand().floorPaise`. Months run to January of 2050.
- **House.**
  - Balance = B2 flows + RSU value.
  - RSU value = the latest `holdings` quantity for the RSU-kind instrument × the latest `prices_eod.close_paise` for that instrument.
    - `close_paise` is **already INR**: `now-history.ts` converts at that day's FX. Do **not** multiply by FX again. This corrects spec §4's wording.
    - The price must be no older than 7 days, or the card is UNKNOWN with reason "ServiceNow price is N days old".
    - The `holdings.value_paise` of that row is never used, because it is the stale manual seed.
  - Growth at the debt rate; no contributions; target 6 500 000 rupees at January 2033.
  - Shortfall uses `levelMonthlyToClose` at the debt rate.
- **Emergency.** B3 flows, no growth. Shortfall = gap / months left, where months left counts the current IST month through `emergencyTargetMonth` inclusive. Round up.
- **Education.**
  - Before January `childArrivalYear`, the verdict is NOT_STARTED. `shortMonthlyPaise` is the level monthly from January 2028 to January 2046 at the equity real rate that reaches ₹1Cr minus B4 flows.
  - From 2028 on, the card follows the same rule as the house, but at the equity rate.
- **Verdict.** ON_TRACK if central projected ≥ target. `finishYear` runs `firstMonthReaching` per scenario and reports the calendar year (null means "not by 2100").

  - Each of `src/domain/sizing.ts`, `src/domain/sizing-input.ts`, `src/domain/alloc-engine.ts`, `src/domain/rails.ts`, `src/notify/report.ts` and `src/advisor/recommend.ts` exists in the graph and is not a reacher.

### Task 3: The goal plan: routes and risk disclosure (pure over the tracker)

**Files:**
- Modify `src/domain/goal-plan.ts`.
- Test: `tests/domain/goal-plan-routes.test.ts`.

**Interfaces (Produces):**
```ts
interface MoneyRoute { goal: GoalId; extraMonthlyPaise: bigint;
  legs: { assetClass: 'EQUITY' | 'DEBT' | 'GOLD'; instrumentId: string | null; paise: bigint }[] }
interface RiskRoute { equityPct: number; closes: boolean; disclosure: RiskDisclosure }
interface RiskDisclosure {
  band: string;                         // "equity 68% of new money vs 60% ceiling, IPS §3.3"
  crashLossPaise: { normal: bigint; goal: bigint };   // equity pot × crashEquityDrop at crashHorizonMonths
  fiYearAfterCrash: { normal: number | null; goal: number | null };
  aboveBudgetSource: string;            // "a raise you would set in your monthly budget update"
}
interface GoalPlan { money: MoneyRoute[]; totalExtraMonthlyPaise: bigint;
  proposedBudgetPaise: bigint; risk: RiskRoute | null; signedCeilingPct: number }
buildGoalPlan(t: GoalTracker, positions: Position[], signedCeilingPct: number): GoalPlan
loadGoalPlan(db: Db, nowIso: string): Promise<{ tracker: GoalTracker; plan: GoalPlan }>
```

**Rules:**
- **Money routes.**
  - One route per SHORT goal, plus education when NOT_STARTED, labelled "from 2028". Education does not count in the total until 2028.
  - FI legs split by `newMoneyMix(signedCeilingPct)`. House is all DEBT. Education is all EQUITY.
  - `instrumentId` comes from `addTarget(positions, class)` in `alloc-engine.ts`. Importing in that direction is allowed; the reverse is not.
  - Legs sum exactly to the extra; any rounding remainder goes to the largest leg.
- **Risk route.**
  - Present iff FI is SHORT.
  - Search integer equity percent from `signedCeilingPct + 1` to 92 for the smallest share whose central projection at the **current budget** reaches the FI target by 2050.
  - `closes = false` and `equityPct = 92` when none does.
- **Disclosure.**
  - Project the normal mix (60) and the goal mix, each with `shockAtMonth: crashHorizonMonths`.
  - Report the equity pot loss at that month and the `firstMonthReaching` year after the shock.
  - The disclosure is always present on a risk route. It names IPS §3.3.
- **The plan never contains a sale.** There is no SELL type at all.

  - Legs sum to the extra.
  - A met goal has no route (Review Focus 3).
  - The risk route is absent when FI is ON_TRACK.
  - The found `equityPct` closes, and `equityPct − 1` does not.
  - The goal-plan crash loss ≥ the normal crash loss.
  - `fiYearAfterCrash.goal` is computed with the shock: removing the shock changes it in the fixture.
  - **Mutation check:** search from 60 instead of `signedCeilingPct + 1` and assert a test fails when the ceiling is already signed at 68.
